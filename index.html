About me

I am a Research Fellow and Co-Research Group Leader in the Behaviour and Choice Modelling Group at the Institute for Transport Studies, University of Leeds. My research lies at the intersection of econometrics, machine learning, AI, and behavioural modelling, with a focus on developing interpretable and predictive models of human decision-making.

Before joining Leeds, I was a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellow at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU) and a visiting postdoctoral researcher at MIT. I hold a PhD in Civil Engineering from the American University of Beirut, with a thesis on integrating machine learning and discrete choice models.
